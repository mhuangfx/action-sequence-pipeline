## Style
Stylized painterly 3D animation, the look of a high-end animated feature film: hand-painted textures with visible brush strokes on skin, cloth and metal, painted light and shadow, slightly exaggerated proportions, strong graphic shapes, rich warm colour. Every surface looks painted by hand over 3D form. Not photoreal, not flat 2D anime, not cel shading, not a plastic 3D-render look, not a video game.
