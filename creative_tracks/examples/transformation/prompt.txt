[Subject settings]
The blacksmith: about 65, broad heavy shoulders, thick scarred forearms, a thick singed grey beard, bald crown, warm brown eyes, a sweat-darkened off-white linen shirt with rolled sleeves, a scorched leather apron, brown wool trousers, worn boots. The golem: the same man transformed, about 3 metres tall, far larger and heavier than a human, built from thick riveted plates of hammered bronze armour, huge pauldrons, a barrel chest, fists like anvils, molten forge-orange light glowing in the seams, a cast-bronze mask of his own face and beard with glowing orange eyes. The girl: about 7, a red headscarf, a small basket of apples; alone in the middle of the street. The wagon: a heavy wooden cart piled high with grey quarry stone. Townsfolk at the market stalls.
Environment: a sunlit hillside market town in warm late-afternoon light, a steep cobbled street, stone houses with tiled roofs, cloth awnings and market stalls, his open-air forge with an anvil and a glowing crucible of molten bronze.
Screen direction (keep it the same in every shot): the street runs from the top of the hill in the background down toward the camera. The wagon comes down from the top of the street. The girl stands in the middle of the street, between the wagon and the camera. The golem moves between the girl and the wagon and faces UP the street, toward the wagon.
