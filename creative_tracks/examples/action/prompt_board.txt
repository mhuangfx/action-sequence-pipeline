[Subject settings]
The woman: refer to Image 3. A young woman survivor, slender and athletic, pale freckled skin, violet eyes, long black hair with a violet sheen in a tight low ponytail, a dark rune tattoo on her right cheekbone only. She wears a patched dark-brown leather jacket over a charcoal hoodie, a black chest rig with magazine pouches, torn olive cargo pants, duct-taped boots and fingerless gloves, a holster on each thigh, and holds two black pistols. Dirty, sweaty, fast and precise. One woman only; she is the only living person.
The zombies: only three or four fast modern runner zombies in the whole film, never a horde, each of full adult human size, the same scale as the woman in every shot. Grey mottled skin, clouded eyes, torn dirty everyday clothes. They sprint, lunge and climb with wild frantic energy. They are unarmed, with bare empty hands. Frightening but not gory.
Environment: refer to Image 2. An abandoned ruined city at midday: a narrow alley between old brick tenements with broken doorways and rusted fire escapes with drop ladders, weeds and rubble, a burnt-out car, flat rooftops with water towers and narrow gaps between buildings. High sun, pale hazy sky, hard bright daylight, every face and action clearly lit.
[Overall style]
Live-action color cinematic film, photoreal texture, shot on an ARRI Alexa cinema camera, handheld energy, fine film grain, physically accurate daylight, real motion blur, bodies with real weight. Natural real-time speed except shot 8, which is slow motion. 16:9. A breathless survival chase.

[Shot list] (9 shots, 30 seconds, hard cuts)
Shot 1 (0-3s): Front tracking medium close-up, handheld, the camera backs away as she sprints straight toward it down the alley, a pistol in each hand, breathing hard, glancing back over her shoulder; one zombie sprints after her a few metres behind, out of focus. <ragged breathing, running footsteps, a snarl behind her>
Shot 2 (3-6s): Low camera just behind the chasing zombie, looking past its shoulder at her. She slides sideways across the hood of the burnt-out car on her hip, and mid-slide twists back toward the camera and shoots the zombie lunging after her; it is hit and falls out of frame; she lands running. <boots on metal, one gunshot, a body hitting the ground>
Shot 3 (6-8s): Fast, only two seconds. From the side of the alley: a zombie bursts out of a broken doorway at her; in the same instant she ducks under its arms, shoots it point-blank and runs on from the left of the frame to the right as it drops. No pause, no hesitation, fluid and quick. <a splintering door, a gunshot>
Shot 4 (8-11s): Front tracking medium shot, the camera backs away as she sprints straight toward it, firing one pistol back over her shoulder; the zombie she aims at is hit and falls; two more zombies burst out of doorways behind her and chase her. <gunshots, snarls, pounding feet>
Shot 5 (11-14s): Close-up of her gloved hand: she leaps and catches the bottom rung of a rusted fire escape ladder, her arm fully stretched, and pulls herself up. <a clang of metal, a grunt>
Shot 6 (14-18s): High overhead shot looking straight down the fire escape ladder. She climbs fast toward the camera and reaches the top of the ladder at the roof edge, hauling herself over the parapet onto the roof; below her two zombies climb the ladder after her, rung by rung, reaching up. <rattling metal, snarls below>
Shot 7 (18-22s): Front tracking shot on the rooftop, the camera backs away as she sprints straight toward it, large in the foreground, glancing back; behind her, clearly further back, three zombies of full human size chase her across the flat tar roof, water towers behind them. <pounding feet, her breathing, snarls>
Shot 8 (22-27s): Slow motion. Side view at roof height; the camera orbits around her in a third-person rotation as she leaps across the narrow gap between two buildings, travelling left to right; in mid-air she turns back and fires at the three zombies at the roof edge behind her, and each of the three is hit, jerks back and topples off the edge into the gap. Muzzle flashes, spent casings tumbling. <slowed gunshots, a deep whoosh>
Shot 9 (27-30s): High overhead shot looking down into the gap between the buildings. She lands on the far roof in a roll, comes up standing and calmly dusts off her jacket, glancing down; below her the three zombies fall away down the gap. <a thud, a roll on gravel, quiet wind>
